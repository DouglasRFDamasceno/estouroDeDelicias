Flat polished graphic mark, tiny-size legibility at 16 and 32 pixels, strong contrast, thick shapes, no outlines, no gradients, no shadows, no lettering, no text, no watermark. Symmetric compact composition filling 80% of the canvas. Produce a single icon, not a sheet or mockup.
